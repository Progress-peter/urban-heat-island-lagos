Method in brief

LST from Landsat 8/9 Collection 2 Level-2 (ST_B10 → °C), cloud and shadow masked, median per epoch. All four observed epochs use the same December-centred dry season (Nov–Feb). Built-up and vegetated land from Google Dynamic World (annual mode). Heat excess removes year-to-year weather because both terms come from the same images. 2027 and 2029 are projections from an LGA fixed-effects model: heat excess = αLGA + β·built-up share + γ·year. These are scenarios ("if trends continue"), not observations. Surface temperature is not air temperature.

Data: USGS/NASA Landsat · Google Dynamic World · FAO GAUL 2015 · processed in Google Earth Engine. Analysis & map: Progress Peter Akpan.
